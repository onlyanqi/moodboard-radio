Behind this room

A softer place to press play.

Rainy Window pairs an original illustration with Window Light, a slowly breathing ambient chord. Try Soft Rain for a gentle wash of rain-like sound.

This first preview plays original, synthesized loops—not a live station. The rain and steam move while audio plays; they aren't synchronized to its beat. Curated live radio will follow when we have a source that permits embedding.

Made with intention

Scene design and code by Anqi Chen, with playback patterns adapted from rrradio (MIT). The ambient audio is generated from sine waves and filtered noise; no recordings or music samples are used.

Your saved room and motion preference stay in this browser. No account or app analytics. Audio is served with this site; fonts load from Google Fonts.
