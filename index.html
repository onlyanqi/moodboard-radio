Behind this room

A softer place to press play.

Three illustrated rooms for focusing, taking a break, or unwinding. Each room starts on a different track from the same three-song HoliznaCC0 collection; the music is not exclusive to a room. Save a room to make it your starting place next time.

The playlist moves to the next track automatically. Add a little rain with its own volume control, or leave the music on its own. The illustration moves while music plays; it isn't synchronized to the beat.
